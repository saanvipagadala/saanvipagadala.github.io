Organism photos

One picture per organism, shown on its card when you click its dot on the map.

Nothing here is chosen by searching for a name. That is the thing that goes wrong, and it went wrong here: a search always lands somewhere, and where it lands can be a different species, a genus standing in for one of its members, a figure out of a journal, or a yellow flower matched to a fungus because both are called lutea. All of those happened.

So no name is matched against anything. Every organism goes through its NCBI taxon id instead, the same identifier the gene list cites, and the picture is whatever the Wikidata item carrying that id leads with.

Going through the id settles three things at once. A renamed organism still resolves, because NCBI keeps the old name as a synonym. A name that belongs to more than one kind of thing cannot mislead, because only a taxon has a taxon id, and Wikidata’s lexemes carry taxon names too, so “Capra hircus” matches the word as well as the goat. And where a taxon has several pictures, the choice is the item’s own order rather than a rule invented here.

src/fetch_organism_photos.py does this, and rewrites the folder. There is nothing to pick by hand and nothing to decide.

./.venv/bin/python phases/1-searching-earth-for-MarsWorm/src/fetch_organism_photos.py

An organism with no picture gets no picture

More than half of them, mostly bacteria and archaea nobody has photographed. Their cards simply have no image, which is what the map already did for anything missing.

A picture of the wrong organism, captioned with the right name, is worse than no picture at all. That is the whole reason this is done by taxon and not by name, and it is why the empty ones are left empty.

Credit

CREDITS.md lists every picture with its author, its licence and a link to the page it came from. These are other people’s photographs, all under free licences, and the authors are named there. Credit them on the board.

Two things worth knowing

This folder has to travel with the map. The pictures are not inside the_map.html, which loads them from here. Send both, or send the map alone and it works fine with no pictures.

Files are named after the organism, lowercased, with anything that is not a letter or a number turned into an underscore, so Escherichia coli becomes escherichia_coli.jpg. The map tries .jpg, .png, .webp and .gif in turn and shows no picture if it finds none. Nothing indexes the folder, so a file can never disagree with a list, and deleting one simply removes that picture.
