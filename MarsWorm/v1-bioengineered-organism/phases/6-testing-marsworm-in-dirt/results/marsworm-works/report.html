The short version

MarsWorm grew plants in Martian dirt with nothing added to it at all, more than dirt given compost and no organism. That is the one result no earthworm can produce.

How a winner is decided: “the worst cup of one group still beat the best cup of the other”. If even the unluckiest cup on one side did better than the luckiest cup on the other, the recipe won, and you can check that by looking at the chart. The gap also has to reach 0.10 g, about one seedling, because smaller than that is too close to call.
