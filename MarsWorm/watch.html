Six short films, two hosts, three to five minutes each. The first is the whole story. The other five take one version each.

They keep it simple on purpose. If one leaves you wanting the details, they are all here waiting on the rest of the site.

  1. The overview One question, five answers

    The whole project in one go. Five answers to the same question, each one changed by testing the one before it.

  2. V1. The bioengineered organism

    Every sequenced organism on Earth was scored, and none could do the job and survive Mars. So the abilities were borrowed one at a time.

  3. V2. Earth microbes at work

    Before building anything, the design was tested in a computer and fed only what Mars has. It starved. A better idea came out of that.

  4. V3. Earth microbes, no plumbing

    All that equipment has to be flown to Mars and mended with no spares. So could the microbes do the whole job in the dirt itself?

  5. V4. The bioengineered plant

    Six ways to farm outside the dome, and why each one failed. Then a much simpler idea: leave Mars alone and change the plant.

  6. V5. A plant on open Mars

    Take that plant outside and the air is so thin a warm leaf boils. So the plant only works if it stays frozen. Is that worth chasing?
