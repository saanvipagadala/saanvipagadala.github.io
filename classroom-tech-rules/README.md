## 🎯 The Challenge

In February 2026, [ORISE](https://orise.orau.gov/k12/students/competitions.html) asked
students across the country a question every classroom is wrestling with: **what are the
rules for using technology at school?**

Students in grades K–5 were asked to **create a poster of classroom technology rules,
with a short explanation.** Eight winners were chosen, two per grade band — and
**Saanvi took one of the K–5 prizes.**

## 📋 The Five Rules

She didn't just list rules. Each one comes with **the reason behind it** — because a rule
you understand is a rule you actually follow.

| | Rule | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 💻 | **Use Technology for Learning** | Devices are tools for schoolwork, research and learning — not games or scrolling. When everyone stays focused, the whole class moves forward together. |
| 🤲 | **Handle Devices with Care** | Clean hands, two hands, no food or drink nearby. School devices are shared, so caring for them means everyone keeps using them and the school isn't paying for repairs. |
| 🔒 | **Ask Before You Click** | Check with your teacher before an unfamiliar site, a download or a pop-up. Not every website is safe — asking first protects your information and helps you avoid scams. |
| 💗 | **Be Kind Online** | Kindness and respect in messages, comments and shared documents, just as face-to-face. Words on a screen can still hurt real feelings. |
| ⏰ | **Take Screen Breaks** | Every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Too much screen time causes eye strain, headaches and tiredness — protecting your health keeps you ready to learn. |

The last one is the **20-20-20 rule**, which eye doctors really do recommend. Rule 4 is
digital citizenship. Rule 3 is online safety. A 4th grader covered focus, stewardship,
safety, kindness and health — in five rules, on one page.
