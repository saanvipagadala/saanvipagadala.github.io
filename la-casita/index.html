Sustainability means meeting our needs without harming the planet. A sustainable home is built and operated in ways that reduce the owner’s carbon footprint and minimize impact on the environment and climate.

My Question & Hypothesis

My Question

Are our homes Sustainable like Google’s and Apple’s offices? Or are we hurting the planet?

My Hypothesis

Most homes are already great just the way they are, but making a few small changes can help make them more sustainable.

My Prediction

Even if homes try to save energy, some appliances still use a lot. By testing, I can find better ways to use less power and help the planet.

Procedure

  1. Pick the Big AppliancesI chose 8 big appliances in our home: Laundry Dryer, AC, Oven, LED Lights, Water Heater, Tesla Charger, Refrigerator, and Dishwasher.
  2. Think of Better WaysFor each appliance, I researched if there’s a more sustainable or eco-friendly option (like drying clothes in the sun instead of using a dryer).
  3. Plan the TestI made a chart to track electricity use. I tested each appliance on 2 different times between December 2024 and February 2025.
  4. Measure the EnergyBefore I turned on the appliance, I wrote down the electric meter reading. I used the gadget for 1 hour, then wrote down the end reading.
  5. Repeat for AccuracyI repeated the test 2 times for each appliance on different dates to get good average results.
  6. Do the MathI calculated how much electricity each appliance used in an hour, how much it would use in a month, and how much it would cost in money and carbon.
  7. Compare and DecideI looked at which appliance use the most energy and which ones have better sustainable choices.

My Testing Method

To make sure my results were fair and accurate, I followed the scientific method.

Controls

Each appliance was tested for 1 hour, with all the other appliances turned off, twice, in the same house, used the same way every time.

What I changed

The type of appliance, the day of testing, the size of the appliance, and what the appliance does.

What I measured

Energy used in one hour, estimated monthly use, electricity cost per month, and carbon footprint.

Tesla Charger

Has the highest cost of anything in the house. Optimize the Tesla charging schedule.

Designing La Casita

This is where design thinking, engineering, and fun all came together. Inspired by Google’s office and Disney’s magical “Casita” from Encanto, I created a smart home that’s full of technology and heart.

AI Named Mirabel

Runs everything behind the scenes with magic!

Raspberry Pi Pico W

Controls all the lights in the home.

iPhone App

Sends voice commands to the house.

Face Recognition

Opens doors and starts conversations.

How Mirabel Works

Mirabel is a fun, Disney-themed AI home assistant. She understands voice commands and manages all home devices.

  1. Voice CommandI talk to the iPhone app.
  2. AI ProcessingIt sends the request to Mirabel’s server.
  3. Smart ResponseMirabel understands what I want and tells the house what to do.

This helped me learn how AI connects to real devices. AI Coding feels like magic!

Future Plans: Helping Our Planet with Green Tech

La Casita is eco-friendly and an ever-growing project! I’ll keep adding green solutions every year.

Hydroponics

Growing plants without soil, saving 90% of water.

Clay fridge

Keeps water and food cool without any electricity.

Atmospheric Water Generator

Pulls clean drinking water from the air, up to 10L a day.

Free mesh internet

Community-powered access.

Composting

Turns food waste into plant food, reducing waste by up to 50%.

Solar power

To use sunlight instead of electricity, saving 2,000 lbs of CO₂ a year.

Harvest Rainwater

Collect rainwater to save water and help nature.

No AC

Cross ventilated homes and trees eliminate AC.
