# Primate Explorer

An iPhone and iPad app about all 694 species of primates — from chimps and
gorillas to lemurs and monkeys.

### → [saanvipagadala.github.io/primate-explorer](https://saanvipagadala.github.io/primate-explorer/)

Made by Saanvi in 3rd grade, 2024.

## What it is

Thanks for exploring the world of primates with us! Primate Explorer is a fun
and educational app that helps kids and curious minds learn about all 694
species of primates.

From tiny lemurs to majestic gorillas, it is a guide to every monkey and ape
species on Earth. Whether you're a curious student, a wildlife lover or a
lifelong learner, the app makes exploring the world of primates fun,
educational and inspiring.

## Features

| | |
|---|---|
| **Photos & facts** | See real images and learn fun facts for all 694 primate species |
| **Habitat explorer** | Discover where each species lives across the globe |
| **Smart search** | Easily find species by name, family or location |
| **Kid-friendly design** | Simple, colorful and fun to use |
| **Conservation matters** | Learn which species are endangered and how to help |

That last row is the point of it. A child who looks up a gorilla and sees
*Critically Endangered* next to its face has learned something a list of names
cannot teach.
