# Zippy Zoom Mini Elevator

A working mini elevator you drive from your phone — a Raspberry Pi Pico W and a
stepper motor at one end, an iPhone app at the other, Bluetooth in between.

### → [saanvipagadala.github.io/ZippyZoom](https://saanvipagadala.github.io/ZippyZoom/)

Made by Saanvi in 4th grade, 2025.

## What it is

Thanks for exploring the world of robotics with us! Zippy Zoom Mini Elevator is
a fun and educational app that lets kids and curious minds control a mini
elevator from their phone.

Learn how stepper motors work, experiment with engineering concepts, and
discover the excitement of hands-on STEM.

## Three motors. One elevator. A lot of learning.

What started as a simple elevator project turned into a deep learning
experience about motors, control systems, and user-friendly design. The film on
the site shows how different motors were tested, what went wrong, and how it
ended up as a Bluetooth-controlled mini elevator.

## Features

| | |
|---|---|
| **Phone control** | Move the elevator between floors with a simple tap |
| **Stepper motor precision** | Learn how stepper motors make accurate movement possible |
| **Hands-on learning** | Experiment with engineering concepts and problem-solving |
| **Kid-friendly design** | Simple, colorful and fun interface for all ages |
| **STEM exploration** | Discover how design and control choices affect real-world machines |
